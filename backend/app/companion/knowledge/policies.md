# Academic and mentoring policies

## What is the minimum attendance requirement?
Students need at least 75% attendance to be eligible for end-semester examinations. MentorOS shows, for each subject, how many consecutive classes you must attend to get back to 75% and how many you can miss while staying above it.

## What is a backlog and how does it affect me?
A backlog is a course you have not yet cleared. Active backlogs raise an early-warning alert for your mentor, and two or more are treated as high severity. Your mentor can help you plan supplementary examinations.

## What are risk alerts?
Risk alerts are explainable early warnings. MentorOS raises them when overall attendance falls below 75%, when attendance drops sharply over three weeks, when a subject falls below 75%, when internal marks fall under 40%, when the Success Score drops by 8 or more points, when there is no LMS activity for 14 days, when there are active backlogs, or when an at-risk student has not met their mentor for 45 days. Alerts resolve automatically once the condition clears.

## How often should mentors meet their mentees?
Every mentee should have at least one completed mentoring session every 45 days. Amber students should be seen within a week of turning Amber and Coral students within 48 hours.

## What does data consent control?
Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, students choose whether their academic, attendance and placement data may be included in accreditation exports (NAAC/NBA) and shared with the placement cell. Your mentor and HOD can always see your records for institutional mentoring. Wellness sharing is locked in this version. Students under 18 need verifiable parental consent, so their settings are managed by the admin office.

## Who can see my private information?
Students only see their own records. Mentors see only their assigned mentees. HODs see students in their department. Admins see everything and every sensitive action is written to an audit log. Private meeting notes written by mentors are never shown to students.

## How are accreditation exports produced?
HODs and admins can export a NAAC/NBA-ready CSV with each student's latest score components and mentoring activity. Components a student has not consented to share are written as "Consent not given".
