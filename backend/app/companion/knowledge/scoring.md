# Student Success Score

## How is the Student Success Score calculated?
The Success Score is a weighted blend of four pillars on a 0–100 scale: 35% Attendance, 35% Academic, 15% Engagement and 15% Placement readiness. Score = 0.35 × Attendance + 0.35 × Academic + 0.15 × Engagement + 0.15 × Placement. The formula was agreed with faculty and is identical everywhere in MentorOS.

## What do the risk bands Green, Amber and Coral mean?
Green (70 and above) means the student is on track and needs standard periodic check-ins. Amber (50 to 69.9) signals emerging risk; mentors should check in within a week. Coral (below 50) is critical: high risk of attendance shortage or backlogs, and the mentor should meet the student within 48 hours.

## What does insufficient data mean?
If attendance or academic results have not been imported yet, MentorOS shows "insufficient data" instead of a misleading number. The score appears automatically once those records arrive.

## How is the Attendance pillar calculated?
Attendance is the share of all classes held that you attended this term, across every subject, as a percentage. 92% attendance contributes 92 points to the Attendance pillar.

## How is the Academic pillar calculated?
Academic uses your latest declared SGPA on a 10-point scale multiplied by 10, capped at 100. An SGPA of 8.2 gives 82 points. Internal assessment marks do not change the score directly but trigger early-warning alerts when they fall below 40%.

## How is the Engagement pillar calculated?
Engagement averages two things from the LMS: your logins compared with the average of your department this term (capped at 100), and the share of assignments you submitted. Submitting every assignment is the fastest way to raise it.

## How is the Placement pillar calculated?
Placement readiness gives 40 points for an uploaded resume, 8 points per listed skill (up to 5 skills) and 4 points per certification (up to 5), capped at 100. A resume plus five skills and five certifications is a full 100.

## How often is the score updated?
Scores are recomputed every night, and immediately whenever new attendance, marks, results, LMS or placement data is imported, or when you update your placement profile. Every computation is stored, so trends are visible over time.
